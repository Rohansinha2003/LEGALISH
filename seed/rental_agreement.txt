RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 1st January 2027 ("Commencement Date") between:

LANDLORD: Rahul Sharma, S/o Suresh Sharma, residing at 123, M.G. Road, Indiranagar, Bangalore - 560038 (hereinafter referred to as "Landlord")

AND

TENANT: Amit Verma, S/o Ramesh Verma, residing at 456, Koramangala 5th Block, Bangalore - 560095 (hereinafter referred to as "Tenant")

PROPERTY: Flat No. 204, Prestige Apartment, 789 Residency Road, Bangalore - 560025 (hereinafter referred to as "Premises")

1. TERM
This Agreement shall be for a period of twelve (12) months commencing from 1st January 2027 and ending on 31st December 2027.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT
2.1 The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 15,000 (Rupees Fifteen Thousand Only) to the Landlord.
2.2 The rent shall be due and payable on or before the 5th day of every calendar month.
2.3 A security deposit of Rs. 45,000 (Rupees Forty-Five Thousand Only) has been paid by the Tenant to the Landlord on the date of execution of this Agreement.
2.4 The security deposit shall be refunded within 30 days of the Tenant vacating the Premises, subject to deductions for unpaid rent or property damage beyond fair wear and tear.

3. MAINTENANCE
3.1 The Tenant shall pay monthly maintenance charges of Rs. 2,000 per month directly to the apartment association.
3.2 The Landlord shall be responsible for major structural repairs.
3.3 The Tenant shall maintain the Premises in good condition.

4. USE OF PREMISES
4.1 The Premises shall be used for residential purposes only.
4.2 The Tenant shall not carry on any trade, business, or commercial activity from the Premises.

5. SUBLETTING
5.1 The Tenant shall not sublet, assign, or part with the possession of the Premises or any part thereof without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

6. UTILITIES
6.1 Electricity, water, and internet charges shall be borne by the Tenant.

7. ALTERATIONS
7.1 The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations or additions to the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

8. TERMINATION
8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.
8.2 Upon termination, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises peacefully and hand over possession to the Landlord in good condition.

9. INSPECTION
9.1 The Landlord or their authorized representative shall have the right to inspect the Premises with 24 hours prior notice to the Tenant.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
10.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably by the parties.
10.2 If not resolved amicably within 30 days, the dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator.
10.3 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Karnataka.

11. GENERAL
11.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
11.2 Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above.
